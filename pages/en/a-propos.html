Who we are

HNet Media is an independent French- and English-language media outlet dedicated to rigorous, verified and accessible news.

Our mission

Since its creation, HNet Media has been committed to covering Politics, World, Economy, Sport, Culture, Tech and Health news with high standards and editorial independence, in the service of citizens.

Our team

A newsroom of journalists, editorial writers and sector experts works every day to check the facts and give context to the news. Meet our team.

Editorial charter

Our journalists follow a strict code of ethics: source verification, right of reply, separation of facts and opinions, and transparency about conflicts of interest. Read our full editorial charter.

IndependenceNo outside interference in how the news is handled.
RigourEvery piece of information is cross-checked before publication.
TransparencySources and corrections are always mentioned.
AccessibilityClear news, in French and in English.
