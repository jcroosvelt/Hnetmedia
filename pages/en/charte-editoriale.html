Editorial charter

This charter sets out the ethical principles that guide the work of the HNet Media newsroom.

Editorial independence

No shareholder, advertiser or political figure takes part in choosing or handling the subjects we publish.

Fact-checking

Every piece of information is cross-checked with at least two sources before publication. Where there is uncertainty, it is clearly flagged to the reader.

Separating facts from opinions

News articles and opinion pieces (op-eds, editorials, analysis) are clearly distinguished by a dedicated badge, notably in the Opinions & Analysis section.

Right of reply and corrections

  • Anyone who is called into question has a right of reply.
  • Proven errors are corrected as quickly as possible, with a visible note of the correction and its date on the article concerned.
  • Anonymous sources are used only as a last resort and under strict editorial control.

Sponsored content

All sponsored content is flagged with a distinct badge and may not interfere with the editorial handling of news subjects.

Conflicts of interest

Journalists disclose any potential conflict of interest and step away from the subjects concerned where necessary.
