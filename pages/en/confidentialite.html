Privacy policy

HNet Media attaches great importance to the protection of your personal data.

Data collected

  • Name, email address and message, only if you write to us through the contact form.
  • Anonymous audience measurement: when an article is opened, the site counts one read, grouped by hour. No cookie, IP address or identifier is kept, and nothing is counted if your browser sends the “Do Not Track” signal.
The newsletter is not open yet: no address is collected for it for the time being.

Use of data

Messages received are used only to answer your request. Counted reads are used only to produce overall audience statistics for the newsroom.

Your rights

In accordance with applicable regulations, you have a right of access, rectification and deletion of your data. To exercise it, contact us through the Contact page.
